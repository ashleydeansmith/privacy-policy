Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 September 2026

This policy covers a private app registered on Meta's developer platform by Ashley Dean Smith. The app connects to one Instagram account, @ashsmith1942, which Ashley Dean Smith owns. It is not offered to the public and no one else can sign in to it.

What the app reads

Through Meta's Instagram API, the app reads data from the owner's own Instagram account: posts and their statistics, account statistics, follower statistics as grouped by Meta (country, city, age group, gender), comments on the owner's posts, and direct messages sent to the owner's account.

How the data is used

The data is used only by the account owner, to review the account's performance and to read and manage the account's own messages and comments. It is not used for advertising and is not used to contact anyone who has not first written to the account.

Where the data is kept

Data read by the app is stored on the account owner's own computer. It is not sold, rented or shared with any third party.

Deleting your data

YouTube app (Google)

This policy also covers a private app registered on Google Cloud by Ashley Dean Smith, named "Youtube Reporting". It connects only to Ashley Dean Smith's own YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public and no one else signs in to it.

What it does: through the YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API it reads the owner's own channel, videos and their statistics, and uploads the owner's own videos to the owner's channel (always as private; the owner publishes them himself).

How the data is used and stored: only by the channel owner, on his own computer, to manage and understand his own channel. It is not sold, shared with anyone else, or used for advertising.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access: access can be withdrawn at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
