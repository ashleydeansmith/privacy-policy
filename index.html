Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 September 2026

This policy covers a private app registered on Meta's developer platform by Ashley Dean Smith. The app connects to one Instagram account, @ashsmith1942, which Ashley Dean Smith owns. It is not offered to the public and no one else can sign in to it.

What the app reads

Through Meta's Instagram API, the app reads data from the owner's own Instagram account: posts and their statistics, account statistics, follower statistics as grouped by Meta (country, city, age group, gender), comments on the owner's posts, and direct messages sent to the owner's account.

How the data is used

The data is used only by the account owner, to review the account's performance and to read and manage the account's own messages and comments. It is not used for advertising and is not used to contact anyone who has not first written to the account.

Where the data is kept

Data read by the app is stored on the account owner's own computer. It is not sold, rented or shared with any third party.

Deleting your data

If you have messaged or commented on @ashsmith1942 and want any copy of that data held by this app deleted, email the address below with your Instagram username. It will be deleted within 30 days and you will be told when it has been.

YouTube app (Google)

This section last updated: 9 October 2026.

This policy also covers a private app registered on Google Cloud by Ashley Dean Smith, named "Youtube Reporting". The app uses YouTube API Services. It is not offered to the public: the only person who signs in to it is Ashley Dean Smith, with his own Google account.

By using this app you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Information is also handled under the Google Privacy Policy.

What it accesses:

  • The owner's own channel (with his sign-in): his channel, videos and their statistics through the YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API. It also uploads his own videos to his own channel, always as private; he publishes them himself.
  • Public information about other videos and channels (no sign-in of theirs is involved): for research into which subjects and titles do well, the app reads public search results and, for those videos, their titles, thumbnails, lengths, publish dates and view counts, and their channels' public subscriber counts and upload lists.

How it is used: only by the channel owner, to manage his own channel and to decide what to make next. It is not used for advertising and is not sold.

Who else processes it: titles and thumbnails of public videos may be sent to an AI model provider (Anthropic) so the app can sort them into kinds of video. Nothing from the owner's private channel data is sent for that purpose, and the data is not shared with anyone else.

Where it is kept, and for how long: on the owner's own computer. Public information about other people's videos and channels is kept for no longer than 30 days, then deleted or refreshed.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access: access can be withdrawn at any time from the Google security settings page, security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.

Questions, complaints or deletion requests: email as.coaching2000@gmail.com. A deletion request is completed within 7 days.
