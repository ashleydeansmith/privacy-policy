Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 September 2026

This policy covers a private app registered on Meta's developer platform by Ashley Dean Smith. The app connects to one Instagram account, @ashsmith1942, which Ashley Dean Smith owns. It is not offered to the public and no one else can sign in to it.

What the app reads

Through Meta's Instagram API, the app reads data from the owner's own Instagram account: posts and their statistics, account statistics, follower statistics as grouped by Meta (country, city, age group, gender), comments on the owner's posts, and direct messages sent to the owner's account.

How the data is used

The data is used only by the account owner, to review the account's performance and to read and manage the account's own messages and comments. It is not used for advertising and is not used to contact anyone who has not first written to the account.

Where the data is kept

Data read by the app is stored on the account owner's own computer. It is not sold, rented or shared with any third party.

Deleting your data
